## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- Unequal hours reckoned from the local sunrise and sunset, offline, with a dial of the day's and the night's twelve hours.
- A rule of any number of hours, each set by the sun, the clock or by hand, with weekdays and one of five synthesised bells.
- Thirteen configurable practices: breathing, meditation, metta, beads, reading, question, thanks, review of the day, bows, walking, gazing, silence and your own steps, plus a short rule for each hour.
- The illuminated manuscript: rubrics, initials in red, lapis or gold, margin marks, vines, drolleries, blanks for missed hours, glosses on dried pages.
- Month leaves with the labour of the month and the zodiac arch, red-letter days, coloured seasons, day rules, the great silence and a shelf of bound years.
- Ready-made rules: Benedictine, Stoic, zen, Hesychast, metta, five minutes and a blank page.
- 91 readings and 44 questions in Russian and English; EN and RU; demo build.
- Community links in settings: report a bug, suggest an idea or follow updates through the StillCraft bot on Telegram.
